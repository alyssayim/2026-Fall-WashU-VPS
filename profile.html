What do you want to learn in this course?

I'll be teaching, but I'd like to learn from students. I'm curious where you all think the web and technology should be going at large from here on out. We live in a pretty pivotal time as far as AI and things go, and I'm genuinely curious what your point of view with all this change has been.

Tell us about an early experience with computers or the internet

I learned how to read because of a computer game. I remember when I first started playing Treasure Mountain, this game where you had to solve simple math and reading problems to advance up a mountain, I didn't know how to read. I'd have to call my mom over to help me read the questions. Eventually I got tired of that so it inspired me to learn how to read so I could advance up the mountain more easily. The music and graphics were very memorable and comforting. I still really believe in educational / learning technology, and my career has certainly explored this so far.

What will the web look like in 10 years from now?

It will probably be much more dispersed, through not only screens but audio and other devices, to become more embedded in our environments and ubiquitous. This could be a good thing if it's done responsibly. I can't imagine us as a society continuing on with the form factor of the smartphone. I also hope that if people want to, they can live with healthy boundaries with the web and networked technology.
