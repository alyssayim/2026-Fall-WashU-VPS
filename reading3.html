Reading Response 3

This is a reading response for the conversation on what a website really is.

I thought this was especially resonant because it was a conversation I recently had in my Web Development class, yet the approach was entirely different. In WebDev, my professor makes an active effort to teach effective, optimized AI use and which LLMs are the best per buck. Although he does this with some reservation—understanding the artistic importance of an informed “author and architect” behind website building—the class is basically full of people paying top-buck subscriptions to Claude, Hermes, or Obsidian. I think the stronger emphasis on maintaining website-building skills as a somewhat resistant act to the rising replacements is more compatible with my illustrative background—the thing that inspired me to explore HCI with visual design.
