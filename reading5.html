Reading Response 5

This is a reading response for the The Forbidden Zone.

This interview made me realize the extent to which my illustration background has been affecting my approach to web design. I admire Damon's almost bare but compelling approach to websites... Unpacking my intuitively illustrative approach to things that should be prioritizing showcasing, navigation, and internal navigation is a big hurdle in my particular spot as an aspiring Product Designer. His inspirations, particularly the Testament, was extremely fascinating to me—everybody has unique shimmering points, which seems obvious, but comparing the Testament to my shimmering points (Giger's grotesquely maximalist paintings, Ghost in the Shell, McQueen's earliest runway shows) really demonstrated the breadth of inspiration among artists in seemingly similar practices.
