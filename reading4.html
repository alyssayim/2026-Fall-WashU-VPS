Reading Response 4

This is a reading response to Eno.

It’s easy to attribute a grandiose meaning to creation, or some way to touch the audience by pouring your very essence into the product. Eno frames it differently—it doesn’t have to be accomplishing something great. He expresses wanting to strip away the expressive voice from soundtracks, creating unpleasant cacophonies with little musical literacy, and creating from feelings alone. Eno also expresses his previous assumption that thought and feelings were separate, independent modules, only to later realize that feelings were simply the predecessors of thought. Another quote that resonated was during his conversation about the irony of the decreased mass of the human brain (15% in the past 20,000 years or so), which contradicted the sci-fi predictions of enlarged skulls and extreme growth in brain capacity. He then attributes this finding to a revelation of his–Eno believes that brains shrunk due to specialization. I immediately thought, what an optimistic view of the human brain. Especially thought provoking in the conversation about generative AI, particularly more than accounts that discredit its legitimacy altogether.
