Writing Response 1

What is a ritual, practice, or routine in your life that is important for your psychological wellbeing and/or fulfillment? Why?

There have been a lot of times in my life where it was difficult to find structure, yet I think I’ve developed some methods to preserve some sense of consistency and enjoyment. I spent all of highschool watching movies with my best friend—the worse, the better. Although watching them alone isn’t the same, finishing one gives me a sense of accomplishment. I think consuming long-form content (rather than short) is a small but meaningful step towards productivity.

Binging movies is less consistent for me, as it usually manifests from sudden bursts of energy (which is apparent in my Letterboxd). A true non-negotiable in my daily routine are long-form videos, ranging from biology and chemistry, politics, and fun podcasts. I watch Hank Green every morning, and I think it’s because I have no skill or understanding in the STEM field. He explains things like the virality of measles or ice as a rock, and I know I’ll learn something new. My targeted subject comes in waves—I spent all of summer watching every single Alex o’ Connor video after my fair share of comparative literature classes, and I think I’ve accidentally become a materialist. I also try to listen to Reuters and keep up with the Associated Press on my walks to class.

Podcasts are definitely more of a guilty pleasure… I’ve had waves of obsession for the Emergency Intercom, Noel Miller (his capitalism series was great), High Brow, and now the Basement Yard. I think Noel Miller is an example of my habit of consuming media with no intersection with my practice… He extensively makes fun of prediction markets and men in finance, which doesn’t really have anything to do with me, but is interesting nonetheless.
