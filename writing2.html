Writing Response 2

What is a human-made creation that brings out the best in you? Why?

My first instinct was to say the city, or a movie, or an exhibition…something that stimulates me creatively. But if I think about the best version of myself outside of output, but general satisfaction with who I am, it would be in the company of my best friend.

I think a lot of my concerns about inadequacy come from performance—a lot of friendships in college feel like something that requires constant self-awareness and inconvenience. The worst versions of me come from this failure to perform, whether its a social interaction or an exam.

There’s only person that’ll trek through multiple NYC boroughs to look at random stores, take a train three hours upstate to see a museum, or tolerate hours of understimulating movies for me. I know for a fact that I wouldn’t see half the artwork, places, and movies that motivate me (many of which were my shimmering points) if she didn’t encourage a visit. There’s the certainty of her being there with me, no matter if I’m annoying or low energy, all the time, and whether it’s in NYC or our rooms, I never feel self-conscious about who I am. I feel like my constant experience of performing at college made me realize that I have a very fragile self-identity, which I hardly had to question growing up with Nawar.
