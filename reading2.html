Reading Response 2

This is a reading response for the new turing test.

Reading the Turing Test, I thought it was interesting how a rather anti-AI article emphasized methods that artists should employ in the AI landscape, rather than rejecting it entirely. For example, encouraging authentic work by penalizing inauthenticity and honoring an expressive submission seems like a great solution. Especially in humanities, focus on structural and grammatical perfection often deviates the real purpose of the assignment—to genuinely interact with the subject or text in a personal way.
